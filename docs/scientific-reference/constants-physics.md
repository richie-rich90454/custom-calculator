# Physics Constants

The universal physics category provides the SI-derived constants commonly used in physical calculations. Values follow the CODATA 2018 recommended values.

| Name | Value | Unit | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `speedOfLight` | `299792458` | m/s | The exact speed of light in a vacuum |
| `planckConstant` | `6.62607015e-34` | J·s | The proportionality constant between energy and frequency |
| `reducedPlanckConstant` | `1.054571817e-34` | J·s | The Planck constant divided by two pi |
| `gravitationalConstant` | `6.6743e-11` | N·m²/kg² | The gravitational constant |
| `standardGravity` | `9.80665` | m/s² | Standard acceleration due to gravity |
| `elementaryCharge` | `1.602176634e-19` | C | The charge carried by a proton |
| `avogadroConstant` | `6.02214076e23` | mol⁻¹ | Particles per mole |
| `boltzmannConstant` | `1.380649e-23` | J/K | The Boltzmann constant |
| `molarGasConstant` | `8.31446261815324` | J/(mol·K) | The gas constant |
| `faradayConstant` | `96485.3321233100184` | C/mol | Charge per mole of electrons |
| `stefanBoltzmannConstant` | `5.670374419e-8` | W/(m²·K⁴) | Black-body radiation constant |
| `vacuumPermittivity` | `8.8541878128e-12` | F/m | Permittivity of free space |
| `vacuumPermeability` | `1.25663706212e-6` | N/A² | Permeability of free space |

## Usage examples

The energy of a photon:

$$
E = h \nu
$$

$$
E = \text{planckConstant} \cdot \nu
$$

The speed of light squared, central to mass-energy equivalence:

$$
E = m c^2
$$

$$
E = m \cdot \text{speedOfLight}^2
$$
