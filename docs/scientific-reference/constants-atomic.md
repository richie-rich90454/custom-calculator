# Atomic Constants

The atomic and particle category provides the constants used in quantum
mechanics, atomic physics, and particle physics, following CODATA 2018 values.

| Name | Value | Unit | Description |
| ---- | ----- | ---- | ----------- |
| `electronMass` | `9.1093837015e-31` | kg | The rest mass of an electron |
| `protonMass` | `1.67262192369e-27` | kg | The rest mass of a proton |
| `neutronMass` | `1.67492749804e-27` | kg | The rest mass of a neutron |
| `atomicMassConstant` | `1.66053906660e-27` | kg | One twelfth of the mass of carbon-12 |
| `bohrRadius` | `5.29177210903e-11` | m | The most probable electron distance in hydrogen |
| `rydbergConstant` | `10973731.568160` | m⁻¹ | The highest wavenumber in the hydrogen spectrum |
| `fineStructureConstant` | `0.0072973525693` | — | The electromagnetic coupling constant |
| `hartreeEnergy` | `4.3597447222071e-18` | J | The atomic unit of energy |

## Usage examples

The rest energy of an electron:

$$
E = m_e c^2
$$

$$
E = \text{electronMass} \cdot \text{speedOfLight}^2
$$

The Bohr radius appears throughout atomic physics:

$$
a_0 = 5.29177210903 \times 10^{-11} \text{ m}
$$
